About Me

SIA · CAS · UCAS

I am a Ph.D. student in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems at the Shenyang Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (SIA) and the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), supervised by Prof. Jiandong Tian. I received my B.E. degree from Central South University in 2024.

My research focuses on developing physically grounded visual and embodied intelligence for reliable perception and adaptation in complex, continuously changing environments. I integrate physical imaging priors, visual reconstruction, and adaptive learning to connect robust perception and 3D understanding with embodied decision-making.

Research Interests

  1. 01

    Physics-Guided Visual Perception

    Polarization imaging, reflection removal, and image restoration under challenging optical and illumination conditions.

  2. 02

    3D Reconstruction and Generative Modeling

    Physically consistent geometry and scene recovery through polarization cues and generative priors.

  3. 03

    Adaptive Embodied Intelligence

    Robust vision-and-language navigation through feedback-driven learning and continual adaptation in changing environments.
